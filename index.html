FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Star Publication provide?

We provide handwriting services (assignments, notes, projects, calligraphy) and educational/document writing support including forms, records and bulk institutional orders.

Are your services legally compliant?

Yes. All work is carried out through legitimate and legally compliant processes with clear terms between Star Publication and the client. We do not engage in any unlawful activity.

Do you accept bulk or institutional orders?

Absolutely. Schools, coaching centres and offices can request scheduled bulk delivery with uniform formatting. Contact us for a custom quotation.

How is my personal information protected?

Your details are stored securely, used only to serve your order, and never sold or shared with third parties.

Do you hold any government approvals or certifications?

We currently make no government approval, partnership or authorization claims of any kind. If official certifications are obtained, their exact details — registration number, issuing authority and validity — will be published in our Approvals section.
